Pressing Details

100 pink galaxy vinyl.

Pressed by Pirate Press in the Czech Republic.

It was important to have color vinyl for this project as it was always part of the design vision. We used Pirate Press as their color vinyl option was cost effective. A number of them came damaged, but overall the quality was pretty solid. Includes full color insert with lyrics and album credits.
